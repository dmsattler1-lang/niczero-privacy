Privacy Policy for NicZero

Last updated: September 13, 2026

NicZero is built around a simple promise: your information stays on your phone. We do not run servers that store your data, we do not have user accounts, and we do not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal information.

In short: Everything you log in NicZero — your nicotine use, cravings, plan, streaks, and notes — is stored locally on your own device. It never leaves your phone unless you choose to export or share it yourself.

Information we collect

None. NicZero does not collect any personal information. There is no sign-up, no login, and no account system. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or location.

How your data is stored

The information you enter into NicZero is saved only in the app's local storage on your device. This includes things like your logged nicotine use, cravings you record, your quit plan and quit date, streaks, and preferences. This data is not sent to us or to any third party. We have no ability to access it.

Data you choose to export

NicZero includes optional features that let you export or share your own data (for example, to back it up or move it to another device). When you use these features, you control where that data goes. NicZero does not receive a copy.

Notifications

If you enable reminders, NicZero uses your device's notification system to show them. These notifications are generated on your device. No notification content is sent to us or to any external service.

Third parties and advertising

NicZero does not contain third-party advertising, and it does not include third-party analytics or tracking software. We do not share data with advertisers or data brokers, because we do not collect data in the first place.

Purchases

If NicZero offers optional paid features in the future, those purchases are processed by the app store (such as Google Play), not by us. We do not receive or store your payment details. Any information handled during a purchase is governed by the app store's own privacy policy.

Children

NicZero is intended for adults who use nicotine and want to reduce or quit. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone. The app should only be used by individuals who are of legal age to purchase nicotine products in their location.

Deleting your data

Because your data lives only on your device, you are always in control of it. You can clear it at any time by using the reset options within the app, or by uninstalling NicZero, which removes the locally stored data from your device.

Medical disclaimer

NicZero is a self-tracking and motivational tool. It does not provide medical advice and is not a substitute for professional guidance. If you have concerns about nicotine use or your health, please consult a qualified healthcare provider.

Changes to this policy

If we update this policy, we will revise the "Last updated" date above. If we ever introduce features that change how data is handled, we will update this policy to describe them clearly before those features collect anything.
